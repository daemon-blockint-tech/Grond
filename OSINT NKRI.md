**Company Overview**

PT Nanggala Kencana Rekatama Indonesia (NKRI)  is a private Indonesian defense industry company specializing in the manufacturing of weapon components, ammunition, and precision mechanical parts for the military (air, sea, and land).1

* Core Business: Development and production of small-caliber ammunition shells/projectiles, weapon components, and spare parts for tactical vehicles, aircraft, and ships.

* Did PT NKRI provide a cyber defence course to Unhan?  
  * No public evidence shows that PT Nanggala Kencana Rekatama Indonesia (NKRI) directly provided or conducted a standalone “cyber defence course” for Universitas Pertahanan Republik Indonesia (Unhan RI).  The documented relationship is a formal institutional Perjanjian Kerja Sama (MoU) between NKRI and Unhan’s Fakultas Sains dan Teknologi Pertahanan (Faculty of Defense Science and Technology), focused on broad collaboration in Tridharma Perguruan Tinggi; Specifically education/HR development, research, and community service in defense science and technology. NKRI delegation (led by Laksda TNI Purn. I Putu Aryaangga Swardika) visited the faculty in Bogor. The purpose was to strengthen ties and discuss potential joint projects for human resource development and research in defense and security. No mentions in official Unhan faculty announcements or NKRI-related public materials of NKRI delivering any specific cyber course, workshop, or training program. NKRI executives have occasionally acted as industry respondents in student seminars/FGDs at the faculty (including programs linked to defense industry), but these are not cyber-specific courses run by NKRI.5   
  * Unhan does have a dedicated Prodi Pascasarjana Rekayasa Pertahanan Siber (Master’s in Cyber Defense Engineering), and the faculty’s cooperation with defense industry partners like NKRI is framed around supporting such programs through research and HR development, not by outsourcing entire courses.7

**Strategic Partnerships and Expansion**

* At the Indo Defence Expo & Forum in November 2022, PT NKRI signed a Letter of Intent (LOI) with PT Pindad. The agreement focused on the procurement of 100 units of sports and self-defense pistols, marking the beginning of a collaborative relationship between state-owned and private defense entities.8  
* The company became a fixture in discussions regarding the Minimum Essential Force (MEF). They expanded their capabilities to include machining for tactical vehicle structural components and spare parts for the Indonesian Army, Navy, and Airforce.9  
* PT NKRI leadership initiated a formal collaboration with the Faculty of Defense Science and Technology (FSTP) at Unhan RI. The partnership focuses on Defense Technology Research, internships, and the development of high-precision components. By aligning with Unhan, PT NKRI gains access to academic experts who help refine the technical specifications of their aerospace and maritime parts.10  
* The signing of the Cooperation Agreement (PKS) with the Faculty of Defense Technology, Indonesian Defense University as part of efforts to improve the quality of human resources through the Tridharma of Higher Education activities of the Indonesian Defense University, namely Education, Research and Community Service.11  
* Helping meet local spare parts needs for Pindad, PT DI, PT PAL, and other defense companies without having to import. Using domestic capital and expertise.4
